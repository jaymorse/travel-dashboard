Connection score: direct = 0, one stop = 1, two stops = 2, summed across employees (lower is better; responds to the country filter). Stops are the fewest connections on any scheduled commercial route from each employee's nearest airport, computed from the open airline-route-data route set. It does not distinguish seasonal from year-round service, does not check schedules or timings, and Sicily takes the best of Catania, Palermo, Trapani and Comiso. Marbella is scored to Málaga (AGP) Barcelona to BCN only (not Girona or Reus), and Malta to MLA. The mild-January button works through twelve above-freezing destinations scoring no more than about 20 points above Marbella (Palma, Faro, Nice, Rome, Athens, Olbia, Madrid, Istanbul, Paris, Dublin, Amsterdam, Edinburgh), two at a time and covering all twelve before any repeats; none is tropical, and nothing tropical scores that low. January temperatures are monthly means (average of daily highs and lows). Values without a tilde are from published climate normals for the airport's city. A tilde means an estimate: from the nearest reference city adjusted for elevation (est.), or modelled from latitude and elevation alone (rough est., about plus or minus 5°C). Verify the specific routes for your travel dates before committing.

Flight time = estimated nonstop block time (great-circle distance / 810 km/h + 33 min for taxi, climb and descent), averaged over both directions; winds and routing vary it by ±10–15%. Many city pairs have no nonstop service — treat as a lower bound when a connection is required. Town → airport is straight-line; driving is typically 1.2–1.4× longer. One employee (Poland, no town listed) is excluded.
